## Expert Selection Criteria

Each expert was selected against three requirements:

**1. Practitioner, not commentator.**
Every expert on this list *does* AI SEO — they run agencies, advise companies, build
tools, or publish original research. No journalists or aggregators.

**2. Unique angle, not overlapping coverage.**
The ten experts cover distinct aspects of AI SEO content production: data research,
enterprise strategy, content quality, workflow design, technical mechanics, AI automation,
production processes, entity optimization, distribution strategy, and content architecture.
No two experts cover exactly the same ground.

**3. Demonstrated influence on the field.**
Each expert is cited by others, referenced in industry publications, or has produced
frameworks the rest of the industry adopts. This is a signals-based selection, not a
follower-count selection.

## Post Selection Methodology

For each expert, posts were evaluated against a 5-point scoring rubric:

| Question | Points |
|---|---|
| Does it discuss AI? | 1 |
| Does it discuss SEO? | 1 |
| Does it contain actionable advice? | 1 |
| Can I learn a process or framework? | 1 |
| Can this help build a future AI SEO playbook? | 1 |

**5/5 → Definitely include | 4/5 → Include | 3/5 → Maybe | 0-2/5 → Skip**

Posts excluded regardless of score: conference announcements, hiring posts, personal
updates, team photos, product launches unrelated to AI SEO, generic motivation content.

## Key Themes Discovered

Across 40+ analyzed posts and 12 videos from 10 experts, seven themes emerged:

1. **Zero-click is the defining challenge.** AI Overviews are producing more searches but
   fewer clicks. Content must be optimized for AI citation visibility, not just traffic.

2. **Information gain is the universal quality signal.** Content that adds novel,
   non-redundant information is favored by both traditional Google and AI search systems.

3. **Topical authority is now an AI SEO requirement.** AI Mode's "query fan-out" expands
   queries into sub-queries. Only content covering the full semantic neighborhood is
   eligible for AI inclusion.

4. **E-E-A-T is the quality filter that AI content must pass.** Google doesn't penalize
   AI-generated content — it penalizes low-quality content. The standard is the same
   regardless of production method.

5. **Distribution to authoritative sources is an AI SEO strategy.** Being cited on
   high-authority publications influences both traditional rankings and AI training data
   inclusion.

6. **Person-level entity optimization is required.** AI systems construct author and brand
   identities from multiple sources. Strong entity footprints improve AI citation
   probability independently of content quality.

7. **The human-AI hybrid workflow is the consensus best practice.** AI for structure,
   drafting, and speed. Humans for insight, validation, quality control, and voice.
