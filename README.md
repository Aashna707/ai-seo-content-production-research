## Expert Selection Criteria

Each expert was selected against three requirements:

**1. Practitioner, not commentator.**
Every expert on this list *does* AI SEO — they run agencies, advise companies, build tools, or publish original research. No journalists or aggregators.

**2. Unique angle, not overlapping coverage.**
The ten experts were selected to cover distinct aspects of AI SEO content production: data research, enterprise strategy, content quality, workflow design, technical mechanics, AI automation, production processes, entity optimization, distribution strategy, and content architecture. No two experts cover exactly the same ground.

**3. Demonstrated influence on the field.**
Each expert is cited by others, referenced in industry publications, or has produced frameworks the rest of the industry adopts. This is a signals-based selection, not a follower-count selection.

## Post Selection Methodology

For each expert, posts were evaluated against a 5-point scoring rubric:

| Question | Points |
|---|---|
| Does it discuss AI? | 1 |
| Does it discuss SEO? | 1 |
| Does it contain actionable advice? | 1 |
| Can I learn a process or framework? | 1 |
| Can this help build a future AI SEO playbook? | 1 |

**5/5 → Definitely include | 4/5 → Include | 3/5 → Maybe | 0-2/5 → Skip**

Posts excluded regardless of score: conference announcements, hiring posts, personal updates, team photos, product launches unrelated to AI SEO, generic motivation content.

## Key Themes Discovered

Across 40+ analyzed posts and 12 videos from 10 experts, seven themes emerged consistently:

1. **Zero-click is the defining challenge.** AI Overviews and AI Mode are producing more searches but fewer clicks. Content must now be optimized for AI citation visibility, not just traffic. (Fishkin data: zero-click at 27.2% of US searches and rising.)

2. **Information gain is the universal quality signal.** Content that adds novel, non-redundant information is favored by both traditional Google and AI search systems. This single standard explains what "quality" means operationally. (Convergence: Koray's semantic SEO research + King's patent analysis.)

3. **Topical authority is now an AI SEO requirement.** AI Mode's "query fan-out" mechanism expands queries into sub-queries. Only content covering the full semantic neighborhood is eligible for AI inclusion. Narrow keyword targeting is insufficient for AI search.

4. **E-E-A-T is the quality filter that AI content must pass.** Google doesn't penalize AI-generated content — it penalizes low-quality content. The standard is the same regardless of production method. Building AI workflows that produce E-E-A-T-compliant output is the core challenge.

5. **Distribution to authoritative sources is an AI SEO strategy.** Being cited on high-authority publications influences both traditional rankings and AI training data inclusion. "Create Once, Distribute Forever" is more valuable in the GEO era than in traditional SEO.

6. **Person-level entity optimization is required.** AI systems construct author and brand identities from multiple sources. Strong entity footprints (bylines, LinkedIn, podcasts, third-party recognition) improve AI citation probability independently of content quality.

7. **The human-AI hybrid workflow is the consensus best practice.** AI for structure, drafting, and speed. Humans for insight, validation, quality control, and voice. Removing the human layer produces content that competes in a race to the bottom.

Selecting 6 strong video educators produces better research than forcing video coverage on experts who aren't primarily video creators.
