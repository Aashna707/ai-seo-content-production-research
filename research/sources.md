## Key Themes Discovered Across All Research

1. **Zero-click is accelerating, not stabilizing.** Multiple experts (Fishkin, Indig,
   Gotch) confirm that AI Overviews and AI Mode are producing more searches but fewer
   clicks. Content must now be optimized for AI citation, not just for traffic.

2. **Information gain is the universal quality signal.** From Koray's semantic SEO research
   to Mike King's patent analysis, the same finding emerges: content that adds novel,
   non-redundant information is favored by both Google's traditional algorithm and its AI
   search systems.

3. **Topical authority is now an AI SEO requirement.** Mike King's query fan-out research
   and Koray's topical authority framework converge: AI Mode expands queries across the
   full semantic neighborhood, and only content that covers that neighborhood is eligible
   for AI inclusion.

4. **E-E-A-T is the quality filter for AI-generated content.** Lily Ray and Aleyda Solís
   both confirm: Google evaluates AI-generated content against the same E-E-A-T standard
   as human-written content. The production method is irrelevant; the quality outcome
   is everything.

5. **Distribution to authoritative sources is an AI SEO strategy.** Ross Simmonds' "Create
   Once, Distribute Forever" and Jason Barnard's Kalicube Process both argue that presence
   in authoritative sources is what makes content AI-citeable — not just technical
   optimization.

6. **Person-level entity optimization is now required.** Lily Ray and Jason Barnard both
   show that AI systems construct author and brand identities from multiple sources. Having
   a strong entity footprint (bylines, profiles, third-party recognition) improves AI
   citation probability.

7. **The human-AI hybrid workflow is the current consensus best practice.** Across all
   experts, the same production model emerges: AI for structure, drafting, and speed;
   humans for insight, validation, quality control, and voice.
