# Rand Fishkin
**Content Reviewed:** 4 LinkedIn posts

**Research Focus:** AI Search, Zero-Click Search, GEO, AI Visibility
## Why Selected

Rand Fishkin is the co-founder of SparkToro and Moz, and one of the few SEO figures doing
original, data-backed research on how AI search tools are changing user behavior. His work
with Datos (a clickstream data company) gives him a unique empirical lens on zero-click
search, AI Overviews, and how traffic patterns are shifting. He is not an influencer — he is
a researcher who publishes findings that the rest of the SEO industry cites.

## Post 1 — Zero-Click Search & AI Overviews Data

**Date:** June 5, 2025

**URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/randfishkin_what-did-people-do-after-they-searched-google-activity-7336469320325050368-rkk3

**Summary:**
Fishkin shares data from Datos and Semrush's quarterly State of Search report showing that
Google searches per user have actually increased since AI Overviews launched — not decreased.
However, the critical insight is what happens *after* the search: users are staying on Google
(zero-click) more than ever. He shows data that ChatGPT and Perplexity are growing fast but
still represent a tiny fraction of Google's volume. The report reveals that AI tools are
growing category-wise but are not cannibalizing Google's market share yet.

**Key Takeaways:**
- AI Overviews have *increased* search frequency per user (from ~84 searches/month in March
  2024 to ~99 searches/month in March 2025) — but clicks to external sites are declining.
- Zero-click search is accelerating, not slowing down. Brands can no longer measure success
  purely by organic traffic volume.
- ChatGPT reached 21% monthly device penetration in Q4 2024 — massive adoption, but still
  tiny compared to Google's daily volume.
- The complex story: AI tools grow fast in *relative* terms but Google remains dominant in
  *absolute* terms.

**Relevance to AI SEO:**
This post is essential for understanding why SEO content strategy must evolve. If AI
Overviews are absorbing more searches without producing clicks; content must now be
optimized for *being cited inside AI answers*, not just for driving traffic. This is the
foundational argument for GEO and AEO as disciplines.

**Research Score:** 5/5.

## Post 2 — Zero-Click Content Strategy (Counterintuitive Approach)

**Date:** March 2023 (evergreen relevance)

**URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/randfishkin_zero-click-content-the-counterintuitive-activity-6957755448426958848-5QoA

**Summary:**
Fishkin introduces the concept of "zero-click content" — content designed to deliver full
value on the platform it lives on (LinkedIn, Twitter, YouTube) rather than trying to drive
users off-platform. He argues that fighting platform algorithms by embedding links is a losing
strategy. Instead, marketers should treat social and search platforms like billboards:
Optimize for awareness and recall, not for clicks.

**Key Takeaways:**
- Zero-click is not a problem to solve — it is a behavior to design for. Build content that
  completes the user's job inside the platform.
- LinkedIn, YouTube, Reddit, and TikTok all suppress link posts in their algorithms. Native
  content wins reach; links kill it.
- This strategy supports brand search: users who see your content on multiple platforms will
  Google your brand name later. That branded search is the conversion event.
- "Build on rented land" — Fishkin recants his previous advice to focus on owned channels
  and argues for heavy investment in platform-native content.

**Relevance to AI SEO:**
The zero-click insight directly maps to how AI Overviews work. When Google's AI answers a
query without a click, the brand that was cited wins *awareness* without traffic. Content
teams need to plan for this: create content that works even if no one clicks through.

**Research Score:** 4/5.

## Post 3 — AI Tools Are Not Replacing Google Search (State of Search Q3 2025)

**Date:** November 2025

**URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/randfishkin_if-you-somehow-missed-our-joint-report-with-activity-7395248123226460160-cdF1

**Summary:**
Fishkin shares research from the Datos/Semrush Q3 2025 State of Search report showing that
AI tools (ChatGPT, Perplexity, etc.) continue to grow rapidly but are *not* taking market
share from Google. Zero-click searches now account for approximately 27.2% of US search
traffic (up from 24.4% in March 2024). He also references a Pew Research study confirming
that users are less likely to click links when an AI summary appears in results.

**Key Takeaways:**
- Zero-click has reached 27.2% in the US — more than 1 in 4 searches produce no click.
- AI tools are a parallel ecosystem, not a Google replacement. Brands need visibility in
  *both* — traditional search and AI answer engines.
- Pew Research independently confirms: AI summaries reduce click-through rates. The data
  is no longer speculative.
- The metric "traffic" is becoming a misleading proxy for brand health. Visibility in AI
  answers is the new metric to track.

**Relevance to AI SEO:**
This is the empirical foundation for why AI SEO exists as a discipline. If 27%+ of searches
produce no click, and AI summaries reduce CTR further than content that earns AI citations
It is more valuable than content that ranks but gets passed over.

**Research Score:*** 5/5.

## Post 4 — Build on Rented Land: Rethinking SEO Distribution

**Date:** December 2024

**URL:** https://searchengineland.com/seo-opportunity-shrinking-rand-fishkin-448876

**Summary (from Search Engine Land coverage of his LinkedIn/public posts):**
Fishkin advises marketers to stop thinking of every channel as a traffic driver and start
thinking of channels as attention capture surfaces. He specifically calls out ChatGPT,
Perplexity, Reddit, YouTube, LinkedIn, and TikTok as "billboards" — places to be seen,
not places to extract clicks from. His 2025 SEO tip: get your brand and content featured
on influential third-party publications that LLMs use as training data.

**Key Takeaways:**
- Prioritize being cited on high-authority, widely-read publications. LLMs train on these
  sources, so getting mentioned there increases AI discoverability.
- Brand awareness built through social and search creates "latent demand" that converts via
  branded Google searches later — the click still happens, just not immediately.
- Medium and large publications are especially important for LLM training data inclusion.
- SEO is no longer a click acquisition game — it's a brand presence and authority game.

**Relevance to AI SEO:**
This is one of the clearest articulations of why third-party mentions and citations matter
for AI SEO. If LLMs learn from high-authority publications, then being featured there is
a form of AI SEO optimization. This directly informs a GEO/AEO content strategy. Score: 5/5.

## Overall Insights

1. **Zero-click is the defining challenge of 2025 SEO.** Fishkin's data shows searches are
   up but clicks are down — brands must optimize for visibility inside AI answers, not just
   for traffic.

2. **AI tools and Google are parallel ecosystems, not competitors (yet).** Content teams
   should build visibility strategies for both traditional SEO and AI citation optimization.

3. **Third-party authority signals matter more than ever.** Being cited by a high authority
   publications are now both an SEO and an AI discoverability strategy, since LLMs train on
   those sources.

4. **Traffic is a broken metric.** The industry needs new measurement frameworks: AI
   citation frequency, branded search volume, and share of voice in AI-generated answers are
   more meaningful than organic click volume.

5. **Platform-native content is the zero-click strategy.** Building content that delivers
   value on-platform (LinkedIn posts, YouTube videos, Reddit threads) without requiring a
   Click is the correct adaptation to both social algorithm changes and AI search changes.
