This is Ross Simmonds' most focused talk on the intersection of content distribution and
AI search — delivered at Profound's Zero Click conference, which was specifically convened

delivers tactical insight on how distribution strategy must change when AI is absorbing

you distribute content in 2025 so that AI systems cite it?" Score: 5/5.

---

## Key Points (From Conference Coverage and Simmonds' Public Writing)

**On the core shift in distribution strategy:**
The fundamental change: in traditional SEO, you distribute content to channels that drive
traffic (backlinks, social, email). In GEO, you distribute content to channels that AI
systems trust and train on — major publications, authoritative directories, high-DA editorial
outlets. The distribution target changes because the goal is AI citation, not direct traffic.

**On "Create Once, Distribute Forever" in the GEO era:**
> "In 2026, the brands that win will act like creators, think like distribution machines,
> and optimize for AI answers, not just blue links."

The "Create Once" discipline becomes more important in GEO than in traditional SEO:
- One piece of original research distributed to 20 authoritative sources creates 20 AI
  citation opportunities simultaneously.
- Volume without distribution creates zero citations.
- "If you're not cited, you're invisible" — for a growing share of queries, the AI answer
  is the end of the user journey.

**On unscalable content as the AI-era competitive advantage:**
The content that AI systems need to generate novel, accurate answers is exactly the content
that AI cannot generate itself:
- Primary research with proprietary data
- Case studies with documented outcomes
- Expert interviews with specific insights
- First-person experience with real-world results

The paradox: AI makes content creation infinitely scalable, but the content that earns AI
citation is content that cannot be easily scaled.

**On the highest-value distribution targets:**
1. Major publications that LLMs train on (high editorial authority, wide readership)
2. Authoritative industry directories and databases
3. Wikipedia and Wikidata (entity establishment)
4. Reddit communities (increasingly cited by AI systems, especially Google)
5. YouTube (rapidly becoming a top-cited source in AI-generated answers)
6. Niche high-authority publications in your specific vertical

**On why Reddit and YouTube are AI citation goldmines:**
Simmonds notes that Reddit has been one of the biggest beneficiaries of Google's AI search
era — Google's AI Overviews cite Reddit threads extensively. YouTube overtook Reddit as
the top-cited source in AI-generated answers in early 2026, per EMARKETER. Both platforms
are treated as authoritative UGC sources by AI systems because of their scale, engagement
signals, and independence from brand control.

**On the content mix for GEO:**
- Thought leadership (original perspective, expert insight): highest AI citation probability
- Proprietary research (data, surveys, experiments): highest citation value per piece
- Case studies (documented outcomes): strong E-E-A-T signal, good AI citation candidate
- SEO content (keyword-driven guides): increasingly absorbed by AI without citation

## Why This Video

The most direct treatment of the SEO vs. GEO distinction from Simmonds' perspective —

---

## Key Points

**On AI search as multiple parallel discovery surfaces:**
- ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Overviews, and Perplexity are four distinct surfaces, each
  with different training data, retrieval mechanisms, and citation preferences.
- Traditional SEO optimized for one surface (Google blue links). GEO requires simultaneous
  optimization across all four.
- The brands visible across all four surfaces gain compounding citation probability as
  more users migrate to AI-first search behavior.

**On content formats preferred by each AI surface:**
- Google AI Overviews: structured, definitive, E-E-A-T compliant, excerpt-friendly content
- ChatGPT: content from high-authority sources frequently cited in training data; strong
  entity signals
- Gemini: content with clear data points, authoritative sourcing, structured answers
- Perplexity: real-time web content with fresh data and clear citations

**On the GEO distribution strategy:**
The highest-ROI distribution move in 2025–2026: get original research published in major
authoritative publications. This creates:
1. Backlinks (traditional SEO benefit)
2. Brand authority signals (E-E-A-T benefit)
3. Training data inclusion for future LLM versions
4. Current AI citation (RAG retrieval benefit)
One distribution effort, four compound benefits.

**On what happens if you don't adapt:**
> "Organic Search is no longer just Google. AI engines are now discovery surfaces that
> require their own optimization strategy. If you're not cited in AI answers for your
> category's core queries, you're invisible to a growing share of your total addressable
> market."

**On B2B content specifically:**
- B2B buyers now use ChatGPT and Perplexity as research tools before engaging with
  vendors. Being cited in AI-generated answers about your category is now a top-of-funnel
  demand generation strategy.
- Brands like Snowflake and Canva (Foundation Marketing clients) have built significant
  AI search visibility through a combination of content authority and aggressive
  distribution to high-authority sources.

## Structured Key Takeaways (Across Both Videos)

1. **Distribution to authoritative sources IS the GEO strategy.** Getting content cited
   on high-authority publications serves as both traditional SEO (backlinks, authority) and
   AI SEO (training data inclusion, RAG retrieval). One distribution effort, multiple
   compound benefits.

2. **AI search is multiple parallel surfaces, not just Google.** ChatGPT, Gemini, Google
   AI Overviews, and Perplexity have different citation preferences. Brands must build
   visibility across all four simultaneously.

3. **Unscalable content has the highest AI citation probability.** Original research,
   case studies, proprietary data, and expert interviews cannot be produced by AI from
   existing sources. AI systems need these to generate novel answers. The production
   cost is high; the citation value is highest.

4. **Reddit and YouTube are AI citation goldmines.** Both are treated as authoritative
   UGC sources by AI systems. Publishing substantive content on Reddit (community answers,
   case study posts) and YouTube (with transcripts) creates AI-citeable assets outside
   your own domain.

5. **"Create Once, Distribute Forever" is the GEO strategy, not just an efficiency
   principle.** Each distribution touchpoint on an authoritative source is a separate
   AI citation opportunity. Volume of distribution to quality sources = AI visibility.

6. **B2B buyers use AI search as a research tool before vendor engagement.** AI citation
   in category-defining queries is top-of-funnel demand generation, not just brand
   awareness. It influences the short-listing decision before any sales interaction occurs.

7. **If you're not cited, you're invisible.** For a growing share of queries, the AI
   answer is the complete user journey — the user never clicks through. Brands not cited
   in AI answers for their core category queries are structurally absent from those user
   journeys.

## Relevance to AI SEO Content Production Playbook

- **Content type prioritization:** Original research > case studies > thought leadership >
  SEO guides (in descending order of AI citation probability).

- **Distribution strategy:** Build a list of 20+ high-authority publications to target for
  each major piece of original research. Distribution is the AI SEO strategy.

- **Platform coverage:** Publish substantive content on Reddit (community answers) and
  YouTube (with transcripts) as parallel AI citation surfaces.

- **Content mix:** Shift budget allocation toward thought leadership (25%) and
  research/case studies (25%) at the expense of pure keyword-driven SEO content.
  
- **B2B framing:** AI citation is demand generation, not just SEO. Present AI visibility
  investment to leadership as a top-of-funnel pipeline driver.
