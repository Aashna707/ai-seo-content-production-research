## Key Points (From Class Central Description and Channel Analysis)

**The 7 New AI SEO Rules Covered:**

1. **Original research beats generic guides.** In AI search, content that contains data or
   findings unavailable elsewhere is preferentially cited. Generic "how to" guides get
   absorbed by AI and lose their traffic function permanently.

2. **GEO is a parallel track, not a replacement.** You run traditional SEO (rank in
   blue links) and GEO (appear in AI-generated answers) simultaneously. The optimization
   signals overlap — authority, E-E-A-T, structure — but the content format differs.

3. **Extractable passage structure is now required.** Content must be written in discrete,
   self-contained paragraphs that can be pulled into AI answers without losing meaning.
   Long, flowing prose that buries the answer in narrative context fails GEO requirements.

4. **Keyword-rich business names have an AI search advantage.** Early data shows AI
   assistants favor businesses whose names contain the service category. This is a naming
   and positioning insight, not just a content one.

5. **Schema markup is mandatory for GEO.** Structured data (FAQ, HowTo, Article,
   Organization, Person schemas) increases AI parsability. Content without schema is
   harder for AI systems to classify and cite accurately.

6. **AI tool stack precedes workflow design, not the reverse.** Gotch explicitly warns
   against buying AI tools without first designing the workflow they're meant to support.
   The tool fills a specific workflow gap; it doesn't define the workflow.

7. **Quality control is a non-negotiable workflow step.** AI-generated content without
   human review produces factual errors that accumulate. A measurable quality gate
   (Grammarly 95+ score, factual verification, E-E-A-T check) must follow every AI draft.

## Key Points (From Class Central Description)

**GEO Checklist Structure (97 points across 6 categories):**

1. **Content Structure Optimization** — Use headings as direct question-and-answer pairs.
   Place definitions, key stats, and actionable guidance in the first paragraph of each
   section. Avoid burying answers in narrative context.

2. **Data Presentation** — Include specific numbers, dates, percentages, and named sources.
   AI systems prefer content with verifiable, citable data over vague qualitative claims.

3. **Entity Signals** — Every piece of content should explicitly reference the organizations,
   people, products, and places relevant to the topic. Named entities increase AI
   classification accuracy.

4. **Schema Markup** — FAQ, HowTo, Article, Organization, and Person schemas are the
   minimum. Local business schema and Product schema where relevant.

5. **Technical Foundations** — Crawlability, indexability, page speed, mobile optimization.
   AI systems cannot cite content they cannot access.

6. **Content Freshness** — Dated claims, outdated statistics, and superseded recommendations
   reduce AI citation probability. Establish a refresh schedule for all high-priority pages.

**On balancing classic SEO with GEO:**
> "Don't abandon classic SEO fundamentals in pursuit of GEO optimization. Authority,
> structure, and E-E-A-T serve both goals. The only thing GEO adds on top is extractability
> — the ability of AI systems to pull specific passages from your content and use them in
> generated answers."

**On the AI content creation process:**
- AI generates structure and first draft (fast, consistent, scalable)
- Human validates facts and injects expertise (non-negotiable layer)
- Human reviews quality against measurable standard (Grammarly, E-E-A-T checklist)
- Publish with full schema markup and entity signals

## Structured Key Takeaways (Across Both Videos)

1. **GEO is a parallel track that runs alongside traditional SEO.** The optimization
   signals overlap (authority, E-E-A-T, structure), but GEO adds one requirement
   traditional SEO doesn't: extractability of specific passages.

2. **The 97-point GEO checklist reduces AI content production to a measurable process.**
   Content structure, data presentation, entity signals, schema markup, technical
   foundations, and freshness can all be scored and audited systematically.

3. **The human expertise injection step is the one most teams skip.** AI draft +
   generic editing = commodity content. AI draft + expert insight injection + quality
   gate = content that earns AI citations.

4. **Original content is the non-negotiable quality floor.** Content that contains
   observations, data, or experiences unavailable from existing web sources cannot be
   replicated by AI. That irreplicability is what makes it citation-worthy.

5. **Workflow design precedes tool selection.** Teams that buy AI tools without redesigning
   their production workflows don't capture the compound efficiency gains. Design the
   process first; select tools that fill specific gaps second.

6. **Extractable passage structure is the key GEO content format.** Direct Q&A headings,
   definitions in first paragraphs, specific data and named entities — these are the
   structural elements that AI systems can parse, retrieve, and cite.

7. **A measurable quality gate is the production system's integrity check.** Grammarly
   95+, factual verification, E-E-A-T compliance check. Without a measurable standard,
   quality degrades at scale.

## Relevance to AI SEO Content Production Playbook

- **Content brief design:** Include NLP keyword extraction from top 3 competitors as a
  brief step. Map competitor heading structures. Define the "expertise injection" section
  explicitly in the brief.

- **Content format:** Direct Q&A headings + first-paragraph definitions + named entities
  + data with sources = GEO-optimized structure.

- **Quality gate:** Grammarly 95+, factual verification, E-E-A-T checklist (does this
  contain documented first-hand experience?), schema markup completed.

- **Production SOP:** AI generates → human validates + injects expertise → quality gate
  → publish with schema.
  
- **GEO checklist:** 97-point framework covers all the variables. Use as content audit
  and brief design template.
